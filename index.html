Why It Happens

Why Your Back Hurts — And Why It Gets Worse Without Help

Most chairs — office chairs, plastic chairs, car seats — are built flat. But your spine is not flat. It has a natural inward curve in your lower back called the lumbar curve.

When you sit without support, your lower back loses that curve. It flattens or rounds. Your muscles work overtime just to keep you upright.

After hours of this, the muscles fatigue and the pressure builds. That's the familiar aching, throbbing pain you feel by mid-afternoon. The longer you ignore it, the worse it becomes.
